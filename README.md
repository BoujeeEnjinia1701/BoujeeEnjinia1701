# Design Molecule

Open hardware for cities, water, energy, health and the places they meet. Every design here is an open, documented concept at TRL 3 (proof of concept on paper), released under CERN-OHL-S-2.0 for hardware and MIT for software.

**Amish Chadha** · [designmolecule.com](https://designmolecule.com) · [ORCID 0009-0000-8079-7141](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-8079-7141)

**12 of 73 designs released so far, across 5 areas.** New designs go public every Tuesday and Thursday. Start with a shared building block like FieldNode or SwapCell, or jump to an area below.
